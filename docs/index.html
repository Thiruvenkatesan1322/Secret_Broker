Secret-Broker A Manifesto for AI-Agent Security No.01 — 2026

Agents do the work.
They never hold your keys.

An AI agent operates through a local gatekeeper. It never sees your real keys, it can only reach approved destinations, and it cannot run a risky command without your explicit sign-off. Below, watch exactly what happens on the wire.

  1. 01Keys belong to you — never to the agent.
  2. 02Default deny. Nothing is reachable unless it is listed.
  3. 03No shell. No arbitrary commands. Ever.
  4. 04Every secret is scrubbed from every response and log.
  5. 05Nothing dangerous runs without your approval.
  6. 06If in doubt, it fails closed.
